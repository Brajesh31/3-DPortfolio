## 🤝 Our Commitment

The 3-D Portfolio project aims to provide a constructive and respectful environment for everyone who participates in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-related communication.

People contribute with different levels of experience, different communication styles, and different technical perspectives. Disagreement is expected in engineering. Disrespect is not required for technical quality.

## ✅ Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give feedback that is specific, actionable, and focused on the work.
- Explain technical concerns with evidence where practical.
- Accept constructive criticism and different implementation approaches.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Make a reasonable effort to keep examples and documentation accessible.
- Credit contributions accurately and avoid misrepresenting another person's work.

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate humiliation, targeted insults, or repeated disruptive conduct.
- Sexualized harassment or unwanted advances in project spaces.
- Trolling, sustained bad-faith disruption, or attempts to derail discussion.
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution, or knowingly misleading project communication.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## 🧭 Scope

This policy applies to project-managed spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other channels where participants represent or collaborate on this repository.

It may also apply to public conduct that materially disrupts project collaboration or targets project participants in connection with their contribution.

## 📣 Reporting a Concern

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Include a concise description, relevant links, and any context needed to understand the concern.

Do not publish private messages, personal contact details, or sensitive information in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, use the separate private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 🛠️ Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers should review reports fairly, protect the privacy of involved people, avoid unnecessary public disclosure, and apply this policy consistently. Where possible, maintainers should explain decisions without exposing confidential details.

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may issue a warning, remove content, request an apology or corrective action, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces.

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behaviour and consider context, impact, and repeated conduct. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is not acceptable.
