# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Accept that people may have different approaches and priorities.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on individuals.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized abuse, deliberate intimidation, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and communicated where practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method available on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed communication channels.
